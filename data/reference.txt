1. input_data.xlsx

1) Generation Cost

Gas Turbine:
Fraunhofer ISE (2021), Levelized Cost of Electricity – Renewable Energy Technologies.

Coal:
Fraunhofer ISE (2021), Levelized Cost of Electricity – Renewable Energy Technologies.

Nuclear:
Fraunhofer ISE (2024), Stromgestehungskosten Erneuerbare Energien.

Biomass:
Fraunhofer ISE (2024), Stromgestehungskosten Erneuerbare Energien.
Bundesnetzagentur (2024), Biomass tender ceiling-price benchmark.

BioCH4-Gas Turbine:
Umweltbundesamt (2020), Texte 24/2020:
Optionen für Biogas-Bestandsanlagen bis 2030.

Generation costs were used to calculate
the electricity generation cost of each onsite power source.

2) CO2 EMISSION FACTORS

Source:
IPCC (2014), Working Group III, Annex III,
Table A.III.2: Emissions of selected electricity supply technologies.
https://www.ipcc.ch/site/assets/uploads/2018/02/ipcc_wg3_ar5_annex-iii.pdf
Emission factors were used to calculate CO2 emissions from each technology,
which were then converted into CO2 costs using the carbon price.

3) Capacity Factor

Source:
Timilsina, Govinda R. (2020).
"Demystifying the Costs of Electricity Generation Technologies."
World Bank Policy Research Working Paper, WPS 9303.

Dataset:
https://energydata.info/dataset/global-data-on-levelized-cost-of-electricity-generation-lcoe

Note:
The BioCH4-Gas Turbine capacity factor was assumed to be the same as
the Gas Turbine because a corresponding numerical value was unavailable.

2. germany-seasonal-co2-v2(by-Notebook-LM).xlsx

Grid Electricity Price and CO2 Intensity

Input variables:
- Hourly grid electricity price (EUR/MWh)
- Hourly grid CO2 intensity (gCO2eq/kWh)

Dataset: Germany 2025 Seasonal & Annual Hourly CO2 Dataset

The hourly values used in this model are estimated/synthetic input data
generated for the purpose of the project.

The dataset defines representative 24-hour profiles for four seasons
(Winter, Spring, Summer, and Fall). These seasonal profiles are then
expanded into an 8,760-hour annual profile according to the corresponding
season.

Therefore, these values should not be interpreted as actual hourly
German electricity market prices or measured hourly grid CO2 intensity.

File:
Germany 2025 Seasonal & Annual Hourly CO2 Dataset
Version 2.0

3. wind

Source: Renewables.ninja
Dataset: Wind - Current fleet, onshore, MERRA-2 (hourly data, 1980-2024)
Website: https://www.renewables.ninja/
License: Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 (CC BY-NC 4.0)

Citation:
Staffell, I. and Pfenninger, S. (2016).
Using bias-corrected reanalysis to simulate current and future wind power output.
Energy, 114, 1224-1239.
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.energy.2016.08.068

Project use:
Germany onshore hourly wind capacity factor was extracted for 2023
